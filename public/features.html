WHAT YOU GET

Features

Everything you need to move files between your own devices — or a whole classroom.

⚡

Lightning Fast

Direct peer-to-peer transfer with 16KB chunks, backpressure and live speed. No waiting on uploads.

🛡️

Private

Your files, your control. DTLS-encrypted P2P first, TURN/relay fallback. Files are never stored on the server.

👥

No Account

Join a room and share. Same WiFi needs no code at all; internet sharing needs one short code.

∞

Any File Type

From documents to videos. Text messages and links too, plus QR connect codes.

📢

Room broadcast

Teachers tap Send to room — one upload fans out to everyone in the room, with live accept counts and resend for late joiners.

📱

Installable PWA

Add to Home Screen on phone or desktop. Responsive UI, dark/light themes, offline shell.

Limits, honestly stated

  1. •
    P2P transfers

    No practical size cap — bounded by device memory and patience. WakeLock keeps the screen on during big sends.

  2. •
    Relayed transfers

    100MB per file, 100KB chunks, text 8KB. Enough for notes, slides and photos when P2P is blocked.

  3. •
    Rooms

    Up to 100 members and 3 rooms per device. Codes are 4–12 letters and numbers.
